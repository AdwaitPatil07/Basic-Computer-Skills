🖱️ Mouse Actions

  • Left Click: Selects an item or places the text cursor.
  • Double Left Click: Opens a file, folder, or application quickly.
  • Right Click: Opens a menu with extra options (Copy, Delete, Properties).
  • Drag & Drop: Hold down the left button, move the mouse, then release to move items.

⌨️ Key Keyboard Keys

  • Spacebar Longest key at bottom; adds a space between words.
  • Enter ↵ Moves to the next line or confirms an action.
  • Backspace ⌫ Deletes the letter to the left of the cursor.
  • Caps Lock Makes all typed letters CAPITAL (A, B, C).
