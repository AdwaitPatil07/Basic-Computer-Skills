🖐️ Home Row Finger Placement

Place your left-hand fingers on A - S - D - F and right-hand fingers on J - K - L - ;. Both thumbs rest gently on the Spacebar.

📝 Quick Typing Sandbox

Practice typing the sentence below in the text box:

"I am learning how to use a computer at school."

🎨 Simple Drawing in Paint

  • Use the Pencil or Brush tool to draw freehand lines.
  • Select Shapes (Circle, Rectangle, Star) to make clean geometrical drawings.
  • Use the Color Bucket (Fill) tool to color inside enclosed shapes.
