🌍 What is a Web Browser?

A Web Browser (like Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox) is an app that allows you to visit websites, read educational articles, and watch videos.

🛡️ The 3 Golden Rules of Online Safety

🔒 Never Share Passwords: Keep your passwords secret. Never tell friends or strangers online.
🚫 Do Not Share Personal Information: Never post your home address, phone number, or school name to strangers.
👨‍🏫 Ask a Teacher or Parent: If you see an unfamiliar popup, strange warning, or confusing message, ask an adult immediately.
