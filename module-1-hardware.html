🖥️ The 4 Main Parts of a Desktop

🖥️

Monitor

The screen that shows you pictures, text, and videos.

🖲️

CPU (Tower)

The "Brain" of the computer that does all calculations.

⌨️

Keyboard

Used to type letters, numbers, and send commands.

🖱️

Mouse

Used to point, click, and move items on screen.

⚡ How to Turn ON and OFF

  1. Turn ON: Press the Power button on the CPU box, then turn on the Monitor screen.
  2. Turn OFF (Shut Down): Never unplug directly! Click Start → Power → Shut Down. Wait until the lights turn completely off.
