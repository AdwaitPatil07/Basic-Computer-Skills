🖥️ What is the Desktop?

The Desktop is the main screen that appears after the computer turns on. It contains small pictures called Icons that represent programs, files, and folders.

📂 How to Create a New Folder

Step 1: Right-click on any empty blank space on the Desktop.
Step 2: Move your mouse over New in the menu.
Step 3: Left-click on Folder.
Step 4: Type your name or Roll Number and press the Enter key.

💾 Saving Your Work

Always save your work so you don't lose it when the computer turns off. Look for the floppy disk icon 💾 or press Ctrl + S on your keyboard.
